Clinic AI Privacy Policy

Effective 1 November 2026

Who we are and what this covers

Clinic AI runs WhatsApp assistants for dental and medical clinics. When you message a clinic that uses Clinic AI, our assistant answers your questions, shows available appointment times and passes your request to the clinic.

This policy explains what we collect through those WhatsApp conversations, why, and what you can do about it. The clinic you message is responsible for your care and decides how your information is used; we process it on the clinic’s behalf. The clinic may have its own privacy policy as well.

The assistant is an AI, not a doctor. It does not diagnose or give medical advice, and clinic staff can take over any conversation.

Information we collect

We collect only what is needed to answer you and book your visit.

  • WhatsApp details: your phone number and the profile name WhatsApp shares with businesses.
  • Your messages: the text you send and the options you tap, plus the assistant’s replies.
  • Booking details: the name you give, the reason for your visit and the time you choose.
  • Technical records: message times and delivery status, kept to run and troubleshoot the service.

The assistant does not ask for your medical history. Please don’t share detailed medical information in chat; the dentist or doctor will discuss it with you in person.

How we use it

We use your information only to provide the clinic’s assistant service:

  • answer your questions using information the clinic has approved
  • show free appointment times and pass your booking request to the clinic
  • alert clinic staff about bookings, urgent problems, or questions that need a person
  • keep the service working, secure and improving for that clinic

AI processing. To understand your typed messages and write replies, the text of the conversation is sent to an AI model provider that processes it on our behalf. Tapping menu options (like choosing a time) does not involve the AI. We choose providers whose terms do not allow them to use clinic conversations to train their models.

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or message you about anything other than your conversation with the clinic.

Storage, security and retention

We store data with established cloud providers, encrypt it in transit, and limit access to clinic staff and the people who operate the service. Each clinic’s data is kept separate from other clinics’.

Where local law requires health-related data to stay in the country, such as for clinics in the UAE, we host that clinic’s data in that country.

We keep conversations and booking records for up to 12 months after your last message, unless the clinic needs them longer for its own legal or medical record-keeping. After that we delete them or make them anonymous.

Your choices and rights

  • Talk to a person: ask for staff in the chat at any time, or call the clinic.
  • Stop: simply stop messaging; we won’t contact you unless you message the clinic.
  • See, correct or delete your data: ask the clinic or email us; we will respond within a reasonable time and as the law requires.
  • Withdraw consent: you can ask us to stop processing your information, though the clinic may still need some records for your care.

Depending on where you live, data protection laws such as India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act or the UAE’s data protection law may give you further rights. If you are unhappy with how your data is handled, contact us first; you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children, changes and contact

Children. Appointments for children should be booked by a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has shared information with the assistant without a parent’s involvement, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes. We may update this policy as the service changes. The effective date at the top shows the latest version; significant changes will be shared with the clinics we work with.
